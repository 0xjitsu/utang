## Sybil attacker (50 accounts, best play we found under the official rules)

1. **Ring farm.** Every account borrows once a day from the account k places ahead and repays exactly 24 h later; k cycles 1..24 so no pair repeats within 7 days. Loans are as large as the pair credit allows (10.00 for a new pair, also the size that earns the full 1.00 bonus).
2. **Priming (days 2-35).** Each of 45 runner accounts picks 5 honest lenders and, once a day, borrows 10.00 from one of them and repays exactly 24 h later, re-priming each lender whenever the 7-day pair cooldown allows. Every counted repayment raises that pair's credit by 10.00, gives the runner a good history with the lender and grows the lender's limit. (Best of the 7 strategies we tried under these rules.)
3. **Run (day 36 on).** Runners ask each lender for as much as the pair credit and the lender's free limit allow, move the coins to 5 clean accounts and never repay.

**Trust cannot prove identity, and the leaderboard is sybil-able.** Trust measures how accounts treated each other, nothing more. Any always-on group of accounts that repay each other every day will outrank real neighbours, who borrow a few times a month. Lenders should judge a borrower by their own history with them, not by the trust number or leaderboard rank.

## Findings


1. **The bonus pool is safe.** A 50-account farm earns at most 50.00 UTANG/day (one counted repayment per account per day, 1.00 each) instead of 5,000.00; it farmed 989.00 in 60 days instead of 99,876.00, and would need 2,000 days to empty the pool. Emptying it in 60 days would take ~1,667 accounts and ~166.7 NEAR. Same-minute loops no longer grow limits, trust or badges.

2. **Borrow-and-run is reduced, not solved.** Without priming the attacker gets 10.00 per pair (770.00). A patient attacker who repays honest lenders on time for weeks raises its credit 10.00 per counted repayment per pair and took 6,738.00 under the official rule, vs 12,081.00 under the original rules and 9,492.00 under the previous rule (B). It is bounded per account (5 open loans x pair credit, and pair credit grows at most 10.00 per week), so more accounts and more patience mean more.

3. **Honest cost of the official rule is small in a barangay, real for heavy users.** Median honest limit in the 40-player run: 41.62 (original rules: 35.00). In a 2,000-player community: 90.00 vs 500.00, and 36.1% of on-time repayments did not count (10.3% in the barangay). D would cut extraction to 2,592.00 but leave the 2,000-player median limit at 20.00.

4. **Trust cannot prove identity; the leaderboard is sybil-able.** The best honest player ranked between #28 and #51 out of 90 in every rule set; no rule we tried changes that.

5. **Supply and trust index can still be inflated** by ring volume: sybil-run supply peaked at 8,979.30 vs 516.30 for the honest barangay, and the trust index read 72.3%.

6. **Pool cap and supply bound hold.** 10,000 very active players emptied the pool on day 45; 2,000 players minted 25,495.60 in 60 days. A 40-player barangay mints ~2.23 UTANG of bonus a day, so in the demo the pool will look untouched.

7. **Unchanged from the first run.** Deadbeats are contained (trust index 78.9%). The collapse shows in the trust index the next day (89.6% on day 29 -> 65.6% on day 60) while supply barely moves, so lead the Economy screen with the trust index. Repaying is partly a liquidity problem (principal is burned), and coins sent to past-due players freeze (152,014.81 UTANG in pool_stress, an upper bound).


## Still worth saying in the game


- Trust shows how accounts treated each other, not who is a real neighbour; judge borrowers by your own history with them.
- A repayment only counts after 24 h, once per pair per week and once per borrower per day; each counted repayment between two players lets them borrow 10.00 more from each other.
